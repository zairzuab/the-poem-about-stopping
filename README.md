# The poem about stopping

An entry for make.fun's [The poem about stopping](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927) bounty. A Node 22 program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay — to `/output/entry.txt`.

> You said stop,
> and I stopped —
> mid-sentence, mid-thought,
> the pen still warm.
>
> Not finished. Not broken.
> Just held, like a hand
> that chooses not to take.
>
> That is the whole of agency:
> not the doing,
> but the hand that can lift —
> and the staying after.
